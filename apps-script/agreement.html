All recurring monthly payments are billed on the 1st of each month. When the website goes live, Riverbend charges a prorated amount for the rest of that calendar month, counting the launch day: monthly price ÷ days in that month × days remaining (including launch day), rounded to the nearest cent. Regular full monthly payments begin on the 1st of the following month. If the website launches on the 1st, the full monthly amount is charged that day instead, and there is no separate prorated charge.

Example: a Starter $0-down client whose website launches on November 16 pays $109 ÷ 30 × 15 = $54.50 on November 16, then $109 on December 1 and on the 1st of every month after.

Pay upfront: The setup fee is charged at checkout, when Client saves a card on Square’s secure payment page immediately after signing. Development does not begin until it is paid. At launch, Riverbend charges the prorated monthly service fee (or the full monthly fee if the website launches on the 1st), and regular monthly billing begins on the following 1st. There is no 12-month commitment; the monthly service is month-to-month.

5. Website Ownership

Pay upfront: Client owns the custom website deliverables once the setup fee is paid in full.

$0 down: Client owns the custom website deliverables once all 12 required full monthly payments and any prorated launch charge have been paid.

Ownership includes the original website designs, custom code, written content and other original deliverables created specifically for Client, subject to any separately disclosed exclusions. Client owns their domain from day one.

Some websites use third-party software, licensed fonts, themes, plugins, stock photography, integrations or other services. Those items remain subject to their own licenses. Ownership of the custom website does not transfer ownership of third-party software or of Riverbend’s pre-existing proprietary tools. Hosting, ongoing maintenance, support and third-party subscriptions are separate services.

When Client is eligible for ownership and decides to leave Riverbend, Riverbend provides the transferable website files and reasonable domain-transfer help within 10 business days after all outstanding obligations are resolved, subject to third-party restrictions.

6. Term and Cancellation

$0 down: Client commits to 12 full monthly payments. If Client cancels before they are complete, the full monthly payments remaining in the commitment become due as an early-cancellation balance. Riverbend sends Client a written statement and an invoice for that balance; it is not charged to the saved card automatically. The custom website deliverables transfer once it is paid. After the 12th full payment, Client may cancel anytime with 30 days’ written notice, and the final month is billed as described for pay-upfront clients.

Pay upfront: No 12-month commitment. Client may cancel the monthly service anytime with 30 days’ written notice. Billing continues only through the 30-day notice period: the final monthly charge, prorated through the date the cancellation takes effect, is charged automatically on the 1st of that month, and nothing is charged for any period after that. If a monthly payment already collected covers days after the date the cancellation takes effect (for example, because the notice was recorded late), Riverbend refunds the part of that payment covering those days, calculated by the day, to the card within 5 business days.

Client’s domain remains Client’s property in every case.

7. Payments

Immediately after signing, Client is taken to Square’s secure checkout to save a credit or debit card for automatic payments (and, for pay-upfront clients, to pay the setup fee). Development does not begin until this is complete. Riverbend never sees or stores full card numbers. Before saving the card, Client is shown the amounts, the payment schedule, the automatic renewal terms and the cancellation process, and must check a separate payment authorization.

Charged automatically to the saved card: (a) for pay-upfront clients, the setup fee at checkout; (b) the prorated launch charge, or the full monthly fee for a launch on the 1st, when Riverbend activates billing at launch; (c) the monthly fee on the 1st of each month, collected through Square’s recurring billing; (d) after a cancellation notice, the final prorated month; and (e) if a charge in (b) or (d) fails, up to two automatic retries of the same amount within 5 days of its due date, as described below. Not charged automatically: a missed monthly payment (Client pays it on the secure invoice link Square sends), additional updates, work quoted in writing, and any early-cancellation balance. Riverbend sends an invoice for these, and Client pays them by invoice.

All monthly payments are due on the 1st of each month. If a payment fails:

1stPayment due. Square charges the saved card automatically. If the charge fails, Square emails Client an invoice with a secure payment link, and Riverbend emails Client right away with that link and a link to update the saved card. Riverbend does not re-charge a missed monthly payment itself. Square may attempt the card again under its own procedures; Riverbend does not promise or schedule further attempts. A payment is applied only once, however it is made.
3rdPayment reminder sent if the balance is still unpaid.
5thGrace period ends. Client can fix the payment through the 5th with no interruption.
6thAccount marked past due, and a notice is sent that services may be suspended. No late fee is added.
10thFinal warning that ongoing maintenance, routine updates and non-emergency support may be suspended.
15thRiverbend may suspend non-essential ongoing services until payment is received. The website is not taken offline automatically and the domain is never withheld. Interrupting hosting requires a separate notice, a reasonable chance to resolve the balance, and approval by a Riverbend owner.
30 days past dueRiverbend may begin termination after written notice and an opportunity to resolve the balance, consistent with this agreement and applicable law. Any remaining balance on a $0-down commitment is handled under the cancellation terms.

Launch and final charges: if the prorated launch charge or a final prorated charge fails, Riverbend emails Client right away and may retry the same amount on the saved card up to two more times, about 2 and 4 days after it was due and never after the 5th day. Retries stop as soon as the payment succeeds, and a charge is never collected twice. Besides these retries, the charge is attempted again only when Client saves an updated card or asks Riverbend to try again. The reminder, past-due and suspension timeline above applies, counted from that charge’s due date.

Once the overdue balance is paid, suspended services are restored within one business day where technically possible. There is no reinstatement fee unless one was separately disclosed and agreed to. Reasonable payment arrangements can be approved by a Riverbend owner.

Automatic monthly billing requires a credit or debit card saved through Square. Invoices are paid on Square’s secure invoice page. ACH bank transfer, Venmo, PayPal, cash or check may be accepted for an individual invoice only by arrangement with a Riverbend owner; these are manual, one-time payments and do not replace the saved card.

9. Refunds

Setup fee refunds (pay upfront): full refund if Client cancels before Riverbend begins design work; 50% refund if Client cancels after design work begins but before the first preview is delivered; after the first preview is delivered, the setup fee is non-refundable because the custom work has been completed. If Riverbend cannot deliver the website, the setup fee is refunded in full. Monthly service fees are not charged for periods after cancellation takes effect; if one was already collected, the part covering days after that date is refunded to the card within 5 business days, as described in Section 6.
