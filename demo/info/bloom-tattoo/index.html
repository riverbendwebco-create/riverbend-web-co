Bloom Tattoo

A custom tattoo studio on Central Avenue in Dubuque, Iowa. The artist and owner is Josh Clicquennoi, who has tattooed for about eleven years, most of them in Dubuque. Appointments are booked online through Vagaro.

About

Bloom Tattoo's website says Josh has been tattooing for over eleven years, most of them in Dubuque, and that he believes in "a collaborative approach to tattooing": he works with each client on the creative side to get the best possible result.

A Telegraph Herald "Biz Buzz" item announced the shop opening in the first week of July, in a retail space on Central Avenue. It describes the owner as a longtime artist who spent years at The Crow's Nest in Dubuque and wanted to build something of his own. He said he was excited about the efforts to revitalize the area and hoped to be part of them. (The article text was hard to read in the page source, so confirm the details and the year with the owner before using them.)

Josh's older personal site, clicqtattoos.com, still lists him at The Crow's Nest Tattoo (169 Main St). It does not mention Bloom Tattoo, so treat it as out of date.

Artist

Josh Clicquennoi (also spelled Joshua Clicquennoi). No other artists are listed on the Bloom Tattoo website.

  • Neo-traditional

Services

  • Custom tattoos, small and large projects
  • Consultation and design collaboration before the appointment
  • Tattoo rework and cover-up style projects (mentioned by customers; not listed on the website)

Policies and FAQ (from the website)

  • Rescheduling: changes need 3 days' notice to keep your deposit. If you cancel or reschedule within 72 hours of the appointment, the deposit is forfeited. The deposit amount is not published.
  • Payment: cash is accepted, plus debit and credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Discover and American Express).
  • Guests: you are welcome to bring a guest or two for moral support. There is seating for them.
  • LGBTQIA+ friendly: yes. The studio describes itself as an inclusive, nonjudgmental space.
  • Age: clients must be 18 or older to be tattooed, and everyone who enters the studio must be an adult. Children are not allowed in the studio.
  • Preparing for your appointment: moisturize your skin before and after, arrive well rested, eat a meal beforehand, and wear comfortable clothing that gives access to the tattoo area.

What customers say

Google Maps shows 5.0 stars from about 120 reviews. Reviewers repeatedly mention Josh by name. Common themes: easy, clear communication from consultation to finish; he takes a rough idea and improves it; detailed realism and half-sleeve work; professional, patient and gentle during the session; and clients coming back for more tattoos. Do not quote review text on the demo without the owner's OK.

Things to confirm with the owner

  • Phone, address and hours (Google Maps and the news item agree on hours, the website lists none)

  • Portfolio photos and permission to use them (none were available to collect)

  • Whether "Bloom Tattoo" has any connection to other shops with similar names (Bloom Tattoo & Gallery, In Bloom Tattoo Collective, Atelier Bloom Tattoo Co.). They are not related and should not be mixed in.
