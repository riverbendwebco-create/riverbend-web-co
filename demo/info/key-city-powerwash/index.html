Key City Powerwash & Maintenance

A family-owned pressure washing and exterior cleaning company in Dubuque, Iowa. It cleans homes, businesses and farms, and has served the Dubuque area since 1996. Free estimates.

About

Key City Powerwash & Maintenance describes itself as locally owned and operated since 1996, which makes it about 30 years in business. Its listings call it a family-owned pressure washing business serving Dubuque and the surrounding tri-state area. The company works on residential, commercial and agricultural properties, from houses and decks to barns.

Customer reviews repeatedly name Elmer Robey and his crew as the people who did the work. No listing states who the owner is, so confirm with the business before calling anyone the owner.

Services

  • Pressure washing and power washing (houses, driveways, sidewalks, patios, retaining walls, stone walls, foundations, chimneys, decks)
  • Soft wash house cleaning, including removing mold and mildew from siding
  • Deck cleaning, staining and sealing
  • Sealing aggregate sidewalks and patios (from customer reviews)
  • Roof cleaning
  • Gutter and downspout cleaning
  • Leaf removal
  • Washing timbers and exteriors of barns and other farm buildings (from a customer review)
  • Repair services and minor repairs (listed on Birdeye and in reviews; details not published)
  • Deck, fence, patio or porch cleaning and sealing

Property types served: residential, commercial and agricultural.

Service area

Dubuque, Iowa and surrounding areas. The Birdeye listing says it serves counties in Iowa, Illinois and Wisconsin, and the tri-state area. A specific list of towns was not found, so confirm with the owner.

  • Payment: credit card is listed on Angi and HomeAdvisor. Confirm whether cash and checks are also accepted.

  • Prices are not published. Reviews mention jobs from roughly $350 to $2,500, which varied by size. Do not show prices on the demo.

What customers say

Google Maps shows 4.6 stars from 17 reviews. Birdeye shows 4.4 stars from about 44 reviews. Angi and HomeAdvisor show 4.2 stars from 29 reviews. The ratings differ between sites, so show the Google number only if you show one. Common themes from reviewers:

  • Shows up on time and gets the job done quickly, with fast estimates
  • Friendly, professional, respectful crew
  • Thorough work on large jobs, covering the whole exterior: house, foundation, chimney, underside of the deck, walks and walls
  • Cleans up afterward and puts patio furniture back
  • Gives helpful recommendations; several customers say they would hire the company again

A couple of reviews on Angi mention a price that went up after work began and a slow response to a concern. Do not use those on the demo, but it is a good reason for the site to explain estimates clearly, for example by offering a written quote before work starts. Do not quote review text on the demo without the owner's OK.

Things to confirm with the owner
