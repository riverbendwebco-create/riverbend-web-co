Big Bill's Sandwich Shop and Coffee Bar

A cozy cafe in downtown Galena, Illinois, serving made-to-order sandwiches, soups, breakfast favorites, fresh baked goods and coffee. Family friendly, and popular with both locals and visitors.

About

Big Bill's is in the Old Blacksmith Shop on North Main Street in Galena's historic downtown. It is open for breakfast and lunch every day except Tuesday. The shop is known for friendly service, quality ingredients, homemade chips and a comfortable room with window seating and good morning light. Breakfast is served before 11 am. The coffee bar offers lattes and cold brew, and one listing says the coffee is roasted at Jail Hill Inn.

Menu highlights (from listings and customer reviews; there is no full menu to quote)

  • Sandwiches and salads: roast beef, corned beef, Reuben, Jacked Up Turkey, Black Forest Pretzel sandwich, croissant breakfast sandwich, chicken salad and cranberry chicken salad, kid's grilled cheese
  • Soups: corned beef and cabbage soup, tomato soup
  • Breakfast: breakfast burritos, Eggs Benedict, omelettes, hash browns, Irish corned beef hash, biscuits and gravy (off the regular menu)
  • Baked goods: scones, bagels, quiches, danishes, Argentinian empanadas, cinnamon donut holes, Brezeln (pretzel bread)
  • Coffee: lattes, cold brew and other coffee drinks
  • Sides: homemade chips
  • Vegetarian friendly, and listed as having vegan options

What customers say

About 4.6 stars on Google from about 486 reviews and 4.5 stars on Tripadvisor from about 172 reviews. Another review site shows 4.7 stars.

  • Reviewers praise the food quality, reasonable prices and friendly, attentive staff.
  • The Eggs Benedict, breakfast burritos and homemade corned beef are mentioned often.
  • Customers like the window seating, the scenery on Main Street and quick drinks.
  • Good for families.
