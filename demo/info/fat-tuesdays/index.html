Fat Tuesday's

Cajun food, cold drinks and good people on University Ave

Fat Tuesday's ("Fats" to the regulars) is a neighborhood bar and kitchen at 1121 University Ave in Dubuque, Iowa, known for Cajun and New Orleans style food: homemade gumbo, jambalaya, po' boys and the muffaletta on Fat's toasted garlic bread, plus big burgers, nachos and a late night menu. Guests describe an old Cheers style atmosphere, strong drinks, great prices and a friendly staff, and the bar has hosted a Mardi Gras party on Fat Tuesday for over 27 years. Catering, carry out food and private parties.
