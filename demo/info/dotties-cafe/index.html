Dottie's Cafe is a classic hometown diner on Central Avenue in downtown Dubuque, Iowa, serving homestyle breakfast and lunch at old-fashioned prices since at least the early 1990s. Crispy hash browns, biscuits and gravy, the Hillbilly Surprise and bottomless coffee, with counter seating and friendly, fast service.
