Fade Babe Barbershop

Barbershop and hair studio in downtown Bellevue, Iowa

About

Fade Babe is a walk-in barbershop and hair studio on Riverview Street in downtown Bellevue, Iowa. Owner Victoria Small is a Capri College graduate (2016) who opened the shop to serve the Bellevue community. Walk-ins are welcome and appointments are optional. Come in for a haircut, grab a free coffee or beer, and catch the game on the big screen.

Services

  • Men's haircuts and grooming (walk-ins welcome)
  • Women's haircuts, color and styling
  • Brazilian Blowouts
  • Hair extensions
  • Body waxing, including back and men's waxing
  • Facials and lashes with our in-house esthetician

Products

We carry STMNT (men's line), Olaplex, Brazilian Blowout and IGK, all vegan.

In the shop

  • Free beer
  • Big-screen TV for sports
