The Holler Inn

Where Small Town Folks Get Fed Right. Downtown Browntown.

Where small town folks get fed right. The Holler Inn is a trailside bar and grill in downtown Browntown, Wisconsin, right off Highway 11 and the Cheese Country ATV and snowmobile trail, serving pizza, wraps, fresh never frozen burgers and ice cold beer. Check our Facebook for live music, karaoke, raffles and events.
