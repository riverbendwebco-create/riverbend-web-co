BUZ Coffee & Energy

Downtown Dubuque's coffee and energy drink shop.

About

BUZ is a walk-up coffee shop on the corner of West Second and Locust in downtown Dubuque, Iowa. It serves locally roasted Verena Street Coffee and energy drinks made with zero-calorie, zero-sugar Bang Energy. BUZ opened on July 6, 2020. Ordering is at a covered walk-up counter. There is also a Bang Energy vending machine outside the building.
