Expert Lawns

An owner-operated lawn care and snow removal company in Dubuque, Iowa. It handles lawn maintenance in the growing season and snow and ice management in winter.

Important: they may already have a website

The BBB profile and ZoomInfo both list a website for this business: goexpertlawn.com. This tool could not open that site, so its quality is unknown. Before pitching, open it on your phone and judge it. If it looks modern, loads well on mobile and shows services and contact info, this is probably not a good prospect. If it looks old, thin or broken, say so honestly and pitch the upgrade.

About

ZoomInfo describes Expert Lawns as an owner-operated business offering commercial lawn care and snow removal for businesses and homeowners' associations, and says it has "decades of experience" (no start year is given). It lists 1 to 10 employees. MapQuest describes it as a professional lawn care company with experienced turf specialists and its own fleet of snow removal equipment.

The BBB opened a file on the business on January 19, 2018. That is the date BBB started tracking it, not necessarily when the business was founded. Do not claim a founding year or "years in business" on the demo until the owner confirms it.

Owner

The BBB profile lists Greg Freiburger as owner. Confirm with him before using his name on a site.

Things to confirm with the owner

  • Whether the goexpertlawn.com website is the owner's current site, and whether they want a new one
  • Owner's name and how they want to be credited
  • Hours, email, Facebook page and logo
  • Service area (towns or counties)
  • Whether they serve homeowners as well as businesses and HOAs, for both lawn care and snow
  • Whether they mow, plant or do landscaping and mulching, or focus on fertilizing, weed control and snow
  • Tree work details: pruning and removal, and whether they are insured for it
  • Year the business started, if they want it shown
  • Photos of their work and equipment, and permission to use them
  • Whether they offer seasonal contracts for snow and lawn care, and whether they want pricing shown
