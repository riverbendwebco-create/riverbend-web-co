Becwar Tile & Marble

A tile store and installer in Dubuque, Iowa. Becwar Tile & Marble sells and installs ceramic and porcelain tile for custom showers, kitchen floors, bathroom floors and backsplashes. It is listed as an authorized American Olean and Marazzi dealer, open to the public.

About

Becwar Tile & Marble describes its goal as finding you the best solution for custom showers, kitchen floors, bathroom floors and backsplashes. The Marazzi dealer page says the store has highly trained salespeople who help customers choose flooring that fits their decor, lifestyle and budget, and aims for complete satisfaction from the first visit through installation. It also mentions free design services.

An Angi listing says the business has 35 years of trusted experience, offers free estimates and warranties, is insured, and asks customers to ask about a military discount. These details come from that listing and are not confirmed by the owner.
