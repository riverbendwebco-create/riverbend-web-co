Sunshine Family Restaurant

Hearty breakfast and lunch at 4th and Central, every day from 6am.

Sunshine Family Restaurant is a down-to-earth family diner on the corner of 4th and Central in downtown Dubuque, Iowa, in the curved corner building with the blue awning. It serves hearty, home-style breakfast and lunch with big portions and low prices, open every day from 6am. Owned by Zoro and Kandi Saliu since 2004, it carries on more than 80 years of restaurants at this corner.

Our History

  • 1890: Jungels and Bade opens at 4th and Central.
  • 1942: The building becomes Karigan's Restaurant.
  • 1948: Rebuilt after a fire.
  • 1972: The Karigan family sells to Peter Sfikas, and it becomes Sfikas Restaurant.
  • 1987: Lyle Ostenborg buys the business, keeping the Sfikas name.
  • 2004: Zoro and Kandi Saliu buy it, and it becomes Sunshine Family Restaurant.
  • 2010: A fire damages the interior. The restaurant rebuilds and reopens.
