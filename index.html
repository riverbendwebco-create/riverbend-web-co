What counts as an update?

An update is one change request that takes up to 30 minutes of work, such as changing business hours, updating pictures, changing text, updating contact information, or adding to or modifying existing content. Unused updates do not roll over to the next month. Extra updates on Starter and Custom are $35 each.

Bigger projects: Premium includes major redesigns, custom features and new functionality, planned and scheduled with you. Custom includes select new features such as forms, galleries, booking, menus and integrations. Anything else is quoted in writing before work begins.

How Premium 24/7 support works

Premium clients can reach a real local person any time, day or night. Critical website emergencies, such as the site being down, a security problem or broken forms or booking, get priority attention around the clock. Routine changes and non-emergency requests follow the normal update schedule. We never promise instant fixes, but we start on emergencies as quickly as we can.

08Questions

Straight answers, no fine print.

The questions local owners ask us most. Still wondering about something? Text or email us and a real person in Dubuque will answer.

Ask us anything
01Do I need to know anything about websites?

Nope. If you can send a text, you can work with us. We handle the design, the writing, the hosting, the security and the Google setup. When something changes, like a new special, holiday hours or a menu price, you text or email us and we update the site for you, usually the same day.

02I already have a Facebook page. Why do I need a website?

Keep Facebook, and we’ll link to it. But Facebook hides most posts behind its algorithm and asks visitors to log in just to see your hours. Your website is what Google shows when someone searches for you or “bar near me,” it loads instantly for everyone, and it can take party bookings and quote requests while you’re busy.

03Do you charge based on the number of pages?

No. We build every website around your business’s specific needs. Your package is based on the level of ongoing service and support you choose, not your page count.

04What’s the difference between $0 down and paying upfront?

With $0 down, your website development and monthly services are combined into one predictable payment with a 12-month agreement: Starter $109, Custom $179 or Premium $349 a month, with the first payment due the day your site goes live. Paying upfront covers your initial website build separately ($499, $999 or $2,499), followed by a lower, month-to-month service fee ($50, $75 or $100 a month) with no 12-month contract. Over the first 12 months, paying upfront saves $209 on Starter, $249 on Custom and $489 on Premium.

05Can I upgrade my package later?

Yes. You can upgrade your service package as your business grows. Any changes to pricing and contract terms will be explained before you upgrade.

06What happens if I need help with my website?

All packages include support. Starter includes standard email support, Custom includes priority business-hours support, and Premium includes 24/7 support availability with a real local person. Critical website emergencies on Premium get priority attention around the clock, while routine changes follow the normal update schedule. An update is one change request that takes up to 30 minutes of work, such as changing business hours, updating pictures, changing text, updating contact information, or adding to or modifying existing content. Unused updates do not roll over to the next month.

07Will I own my website?

Paying upfront, you owns the custom website deliverables once the build fee is paid in full. On $0 down, you own the custom website deliverables after all 12 required monthly payments are complete. Custom website deliverables means the design, code, written content and images Riverbend creates for your website. You own your domain from day one. Hosting, ongoing maintenance, support and third-party subscriptions are separate services and are not part of ownership. If you cancel or moves the website elsewhere after ownership, Riverbend provides a complete copy of the site files and helps transfer the domain. Hosting, maintenance and support end when service ends.

08What if I need to cancel?

Pay upfront: no 12-month contract. The monthly service is month-to-month and you may cancel anytime with 30 days’ notice. $0 down: 12-month agreement, then month-to-month with 30 days’ notice. If you cancel before all 12 payments are made, the remaining payments of the 12-month agreement are due. Once they are paid, the custom website deliverables are yours. Your domain stays yours either way.

09How fast can my site go live?

Our target is 7 business days. Send us your logo, hours and menu or services within 5 days of signing, and you’ll see a live preview with your real name and info within 3 to 5 business days. Once you approve it, we connect your domain and launch.

10How do I pay?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (processed securely through Square), ACH bank transfer, Venmo and PayPal. Your plan and payment schedule are spelled out in plain English in your service agreement before anything is due.

11What if I don’t have good photos or a logo?

We can work with phone pictures and clean them up, use quality stock photos where it makes sense, or set up a quick local photo session. No logo yet? We can put together a simple, clean wordmark so your site looks professional on day one.
